# Google Messages SMS Bulk Automator (Chrome Extension Plugin)

A Chrome extension plugin that runs **directly inside your everyday Chrome browser** alongside your active [Google Messages Web](https://messages.google.com/web) tab.

---

## 🌟 Why This Plugin is Better

* **Zero QR Code Scanning on External Servers**: Automatically uses your existing Google Messages pairing session in your browser.
* **No Server/EC2/Docker Needed**: Runs 100% locally and privately on your computer.
* **Side Panel Dashboard**: Sits comfortably on the right side of your Google Messages conversation list.
* **Smart Anti-Spam Delays**: Configurable randomized delays (e.g. 15s–45s) between messages to keep sending human-like.
* **Dynamic Tag Templating**: Supports `{name}`, `{phone}`, and any custom spreadsheet column (`{company}`, etc.).
* **Live Monitoring & Controls**: Real-time progress bar, countdown timers, table status tracking, and CSV export.

## 🚀 How to Install in Chrome (2-Click Setup)

1. Open **Google Chrome** on your computer.
2. Go to the extensions management page:
   ```
   chrome://extensions
   ```
3. In the top right corner, turn **ON** the toggle for **"Developer mode"**.
4. Click the button in the top-left: **"Load unpacked"**.
5. Select this folder:
   ```
   C:\Users\wiznext\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\google_messages_chrome_extension
   ```
6. That's it! The extension is now installed.

## 📱 How to Use

1. Open [Google Messages Web](https://messages.google.com/web) in a tab.
2. Click the extension icon (puzzle piece icon &gt; pin **Google Messages SMS Bulk Automator**) or click its icon in the top toolbar to open the **Side Panel**.
3. **Import Contacts**: Drag & drop your CSV file or paste numbers.
4. **Compose Message**: Type your message with dynamic tags (e.g. `Hello {name}, your order with {company} is ready!`).
5. **Set Delays**: Choose your random delay interval (default: 15s–45s).
6. Click **"Start Campaign"** & enjoy automated sending right from your browser!
